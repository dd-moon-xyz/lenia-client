# LTR559 light and proximity sensor

The Pimoroni Multi-Sensor Stick includes an LTR559 light/proximity sensor at I²C address `0x23`.

The live client does not open, calibrate, or poll this sensor. [AI Camera person detection](imx500.md#person-proximity-control) supplies proximity input in circle mode; the LTR559 is not used as a fallback.

See [Multi-Sensor Stick wiring](../wiring/multi-sensor-stick.md) for connections and I²C checks.

Source: [Pimoroni Multi-Sensor Stick specifications](https://shop.pimoroni.com/products/multi-sensor-stick).
